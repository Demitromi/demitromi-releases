# DEMITROMI Founder Preview Privacy Notice

Version: FP-2026-10-01-v1
Effective: October 1, 2026, for the DEMITROMI Founder Preview.

This Privacy Notice describes the current Founder Preview's data handling. The provider/operator is Michael Dimitrov, operating under the DEMITROMI product brand. Project Atlas may appear in technical paths or internal component names.

For privacy questions, support-data requests, or incident reports, contact demitromi.support@gmail.com.

## Documents processed on your computer

Current Founder Preview Compose workflows process the PDF and image files you select locally on your Windows PC.

Processing may create local working copies, page previews, edited output, recovery journals, job manifests, logs, temporary files, and staging files. Fill & Sign inputs can include text, signatures, and marks that you add.

For current Compose workflows:

- DEMITROMI does not intentionally transmit document contents to a DEMITROMI server.
- DEMITROMI does not provide cloud storage for user documents.
- DEMITROMI does not collect behavioral analytics by default.
- DEMITROMI does not automatically transmit filenames, document titles, or document contents for behavior tracking.

The local recent-file list and local diagnostics can contain filenames, paths, timestamps, and other device-local identifiers.

This does not mean Windows, a sync provider, security software, framework components, or other software on the PC never uses the network. Voluntary support sharing and user-selected cloud-synced folders are separate from DEMITROMI automatically uploading a document.

## Local storage and recent history

DEMITROMI uses local application-data paths that currently retain the internal Atlas technical name, including folders such as:

- `Atlas/Workspaces`
- `Atlas/Recent`
- `Atlas/FounderPreview`

Temporary output may also be staged beside a chosen destination.

The default output location uses the Windows Documents known folder under an `Atlas` subfolder. Windows may redirect Documents to OneDrive or another sync provider. Files saved to a synced location are subject to that provider's settings and privacy practices.

Recent activity is stored locally in `Atlas/Recent/activity-v1.json`. It can include file paths, Power identifiers, and UTC last-opened times. The list is bounded and older entries are removed as needed. The recent list does not contain document contents or saved editor state.

Using **Clear history** clears the recent-activity list. It does not delete the underlying documents, saved output, workspaces, diagnostics, backups, or recovery data.

The Founder Preview does not currently provide a comprehensive automatic purge of all local DEMITROMI state. Removing the app may not remove files stored outside the Windows package data folder, including data under the local `Atlas` application-data root. Users may delete those local files manually when they no longer need them.

## Founder Preview acceptance record

Before meaningful preview use, DEMITROMI requires the user to confirm that they are at least 18 years old and located in the United States and to agree to the Founder Preview Terms and this Privacy Notice.

The acceptance record is stored locally on the PC and contains:

- a schema version;
- the exact accepted Terms version;
- the exact accepted Privacy Notice version;
- the UTC acceptance time;
- whether eligibility was attested; and
- whether agreement was accepted.

The current flow does not create an account and does not transmit this local acceptance record to DEMITROMI.

If either legal-document version changes, the app can require acceptance again.

## Logs and diagnostics

Local diagnostics may contain file paths, application or worker locations, timestamps, error messages, exception details, and other identifying local information. They are not guaranteed to be anonymous.

Diagnostics stay local unless the user deliberately sends or otherwise shares them. Inspect and redact diagnostic material before sending it if it contains information you do not want to disclose.

## Voluntary support submissions

A user may deliberately send DEMITROMI screenshots, logs, error details, sample files, or bug reports. Email submissions also disclose the sender address and whatever information the user includes.

Use sanitized or redacted samples where practical, and do not send confidential third-party material you are not authorized to disclose.

Users retain ownership of submitted documents. Support material is used for support, debugging, security investigation, and product improvement; it is not sold or used for advertising.

A support issue is considered closed when it has been resolved, the submitting person says no further assistance is needed, or there has been no activity on the issue for 30 days.

During the Founder Preview, DEMITROMI's default operational target is to delete support messages, attachments, and other support material under its control approximately 90 days after the issue is closed. This process may be handled manually. A sanitized reproduction artifact may be retained longer for engineering when personal or sensitive information has been removed as reasonably practical.

A user may request earlier deletion of support material. DEMITROMI will honor a reasonable request where practical, subject to legitimate security, legal, fraud-prevention, or similar needs.

Deleting material under DEMITROMI's control does not guarantee immediate deletion of every copy in an email provider's backup systems or other third-party infrastructure.

The support address currently uses Gmail. Email delivery and storage therefore involve Google's mail infrastructure and its applicable service terms and privacy practices.

## Communications

Founder Preview communications may include build availability, known issues, feedback requests, program status, security notices, important update information, and Terms or Privacy changes.

## Updates and network access

When DEMITROMI is installed using a supported App Installer distribution channel, Windows App Installer may contact the configured update host to check for and download newer signed packages.

Those update requests are handled by Windows and the configured distribution provider. DEMITROMI does not intentionally attach document contents, filenames, document titles, or local recent-history data to update checks.

The DEMITROMI package includes Windows/runtime/framework and third-party components that may have their own update or diagnostic behavior. Their inclusion does not mean every optional feature executes or sends data during normal Compose use.

## Future accounts, entitlements, and paid services

Accounts, authentication services, payments, hosted document storage, and commercial gating are not part of the current offline Founder Preview unless DEMITROMI explicitly introduces them in a later build.

If account features are introduced, DEMITROMI will update this Privacy Notice before collecting new account data. The intended minimum account-data approach is to collect only information needed for sign-in, entitlements, authorized devices, legal acceptance, and account lifecycle functions.

The current product direction does not require a home address, phone number, employer, or full birth date for ordinary Free use.

Future hosted AI, cloud processing, storage, payment, or third-party metered services will require a separate data-flow decision and appropriate disclosure before use.
